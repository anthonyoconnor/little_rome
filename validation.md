# Validation record

Validated locally in automated Google Chrome at 1440×900. No human playtest was required or used.

## Playable loop

- `npm.cmd test`: 22 passing checks, approximately 1.3 seconds. These cover construction accounting, access, alternatives, physical resource conservation, delivery distance, crop care, recovery, winter reserves, departures, deterministic speed, rewind and exact retry.
- `npm.cmd run test:browser`: passes placement/move/undo, camera and sound controls, Go, pause, 1×/4×, inspection, keyboard timeline scrubbing, a complete year, outcome, revise/retry, collapse, and the three challenge examples.
- `npm.cmd run build`: passes. Vite reports the expected large Three.js bundle warning; no compilation errors.
- The default example finishes with three occupied homes, a minimum 22.55 days of food and 7.35 days of water. The dry-summer example with its second well also succeeds. No starting provisions are duplicated by retry or rewind.

## Render and visual checks

`npm.cmd run test:visual` captures the actual running game in planning, spring, summer, autumn, drought and winter, plus a selected departure, abandoned houses, an alternate camera, a close view and a 900×720 planning window. It checks nonblank rendered pixels, imported Blender animation tracks, movement between two spring frames, frozen state/weather while paused, matching calendar labels and restored plants after canceling a construction preview. Browser errors fail the check.

Latest steady winter measurements on this machine:

| Playback | Median frame | 95th percentile |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Paused | 28.4 ms | 32.2 ms |
| 1× | 32.5 ms | 45.9 ms |
| 4× | 32.1 ms | 43.4 ms |

The target is a median below 33 ms at 1440×900 in each mode. These are measured local results in the steady winter town view, excluding startup and accelerated test seeks, not a promise of the same performance on other machines. Secondary reflections, shadows and contact shading are cached. Building/material updates run only when the recorded state changes; residents and rain continue to interpolate between simulation ticks. Resident poses are cached when paused. Blender exports lower detail character meshes for town distance; the browser merges their material pieces, while close inspection retains detailed surfaces. Fully buried cliff faces were removed from the Blender export.

## Resident refinement

The supplementary [close-up concept](concept_art/people/resident-concept-v1.png) is implemented through distinct heads, hair and clothing profiles, continuous skinned bodies, folded shawls, belts, sandals and open containers. The editable rig has 16 bones and its imported walk has 17 animation tracks. Knee bend, heel/toe roll, weighted carrying, collection, pouring, hoe work, reach-and-gather harvesting, breathing and blinking follow recorded simulation time. **Look closer** follows the selected resident and chooses a view around nearby buildings.

`npm.cmd run test:residents` passes all eight actual job/activity captures through browser automation: tending, collection, water carrying, rest, home pouring, crop watering, harvest and food carrying. It verifies hand targets, visible contents against carried resources, water reaching its home receiver or field soil, stance-foot height and swing lift, frozen poses while paused, and exact restoration of joints, props and contents after rewind. The latest 75-frame gait sample has a maximum stance-ankle deviation of 0.0025 model units against a 0.006 tolerance. A harvest sequence covers 1.125 seconds and checks meaningful hand travel from crops toward the basket. Temporary stills, `artifacts/resident-report.json` and a native browser video at `artifacts/residents-in-motion.webm` are generated by the command. These checks complement the full-year, retry and seasonal checks above.

Read-only close-up critique improved from 5.5/10 to 7.5/10 through several correction rounds. The final review found the earlier garment holes, oversized shoulders and impossible pouring contact resolved, with no major broken geometry or prop contact in the inspected pouring, harvest and food-carrying views. The harvest pair demonstrates pose change; it does not prove smooth timing between samples. Remaining fidelity work is softer facial volume and anatomy, less patterned hair and more natural cloth folds. The people are still more stylized than the concept. Winter town integration and normal-view performance were checked separately.

## Evidence and limits

The numbered [progress captures](progress/README.md) preserve one selected image per major iteration. Temporary captures and `artifacts/visual-report.json` can be regenerated by the visual command. Editable models, packed textures and the articulated walk cycle are saved in `assets/little-rome.blend`; the browser loads the exported GLB.

Town reviews compare the six original concept images; the user-requested resident sheet supplements them for close character work. Visual fidelity is assessed separately from mechanical correctness. The M3 town review scored 8.0/10 after correcting the planting and placement preview, and found no important new visual delivery blocker across the six states, alternate and close views, departure and abandonment. No cliff holes appeared in the visible faces after optimization. Remaining town polish includes richer lighting/material variation, less regular cliff edges and small rocks, and more visible neglect at abandoned homes. The M4 resident score is separate, as recorded above. This is not a claim of exact concept-art parity.

Automated checks do not establish enjoyment. Cross-browser compatibility beyond the installed Chrome and mobile touch interaction have not been validated.
